## 0. What this release is

A **minor release under the 0.x policy**, because of `links = "sqlite3"`: a crate that depends on `rusqlite` 0.41 directly cannot be combined with `localcache 0.27`. Nothing else changes for users: no API, stored byte, schema, feature, error text or MSRV (1.89). Nothing here tags, publishes or sets the date.

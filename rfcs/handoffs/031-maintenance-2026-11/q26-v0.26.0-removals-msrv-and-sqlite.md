# Q26 — v0.26.0: two features removed, the MSRV raised to 1.89, SQLite 3.54.1

Authorized by the owner on 2026-11-07 (`ROADMAP.md`, "After v0.25.0 — decisions by the owner", items 1 to 3), after reviews 099 to 105. The removals were announced in v0.25.1 (released 2026-11-07). The MSRV raise is announced in the release that makes it (RFC 023 R3.1). **This is a breaking release under the 0.x policy (minor).** Nothing here sets the date, tags or publishes; the owner authorizes the date after the release candidate.
Precedent: `rfcs/handoffs/029-encryption-integrity/` (a breaking release with an upgrade list), `rfcs/handoffs/031-maintenance-2026-11/v0.25.1-release-preparation.md` (the verification table), the patches of request 078: `.git-exclude/tmp/q23/logs/rusqlite-0.41.1-sha2-criterion.patch` (a starting point, not a substitute for doing it again on the current tree).
Format of the review requests: `rfcs/handoffs/025-error-taxonomy-and-input-validation/implementation-handoff.md` § 6.
Authorship: high-capability model.

## 0. What is in and what is not

**In** (one release, v0.26.0):

1. **Remove the `opentelemetry` feature**, with its optional dependencies `opentelemetry` and `tracing-opentelemetry`. The `tracing` feature stays.
2. **Remove the `async-std` feature**, its runtime backend, its dependency, and the two `deny.toml` exceptions that exist only because of it (`RUSTSEC-2025-0052`, `RUSTSEC-2026-0441`). `async` (Tokio) and `smol` stay.
3. **Raise the MSRV from 1.85 to 1.89** (RFC 023 R3.1, a security necessity: the fix for CVE-2026-119375 exists only in SQLite 3.54.x, which comes with `rusqlite 0.41`, which declares Rust 1.89).
4. **`rusqlite 0.41`** (bundled SQLite 3.54.1, `libsqlite3-sys 0.39.1`), **`sha2 0.11`** (development only), **`criterion 0.8`** (development only).

**Out:** `aes-gcm 0.11` (its own patch, v0.26.1, handed off after this release), any new API, any change to the stored format or the schema, the JSON float question, the storage crate (closed: no), anything to do with 1.0.
